This week
Build your town. Place each home and shop from your hand, then connect them with roads. Doors must face a road (Turn rotates a building). Homes want a shop: put them close to it. On-time trips earn more road. Crossing sets a stop sign, light or roundabout where roads meet. Avenue builds or upgrades to 4 lanes. Bus draws a bus line from stop to stop. Highway builds an elevated road over streets, with ramps at its ends. Shops need a road to a HIGHWAY sign at the map's edge so trucks can restock them. Erase picks a building back up.
